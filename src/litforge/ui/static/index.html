              Quick guide
              
  
              
    
              
      
              1 · Find papers
              
      
              Click + New and choose how to start:
              
      
                
        
              • Similar: a DOI, arXiv id, PDF or abstract. LitForge follows its references, the papers citing it, and
          papers close in meaning.
                • 
        
              • Search: keyword queries, one per line.
                • 
        
              • Import list: a .csv, .bib or .ris export from Elicit, Zotero and similar tools.
                • 
        
              • My sources: PDFs and documents you already have, or links to articles; each is matched to its paper and its text is extracted. Unless
          you untick Then export, the notes are exported right after, for the papers chosen under Export.
                • 
      
              
      
              Each search belongs to a project (one literature review). Searches are listed under Recent.
              
    
              
    
              
      
              2 · Read the scores
              
      
              
        
              Relevance
              How close the paper is to your topic: text meaning, shared
          references and citation links.
              
        
              Importance
              Field-normalized citations, centrality in this set, recent
          attention and venue.
              
        
              Review priority
              The two combined; the list is sorted by it by default.
              
      
              
      
              Badges: new not in the project before · pdf PDF in
        the library · open free copy online · seed the paper
        you started from. Click a paper to see the breakdown, abstract and full text.
              
    
              
    
              
      
              3 · Mark what you keep
              
      
              ✓ relevant, ✗ not relevant. Shift-click marks a range; the bar above the list marks many at once. Marks belong to
        the project and survive new searches. More like my ✓ papers searches again from everything you kept.
              
    
              
    
              
      
              4 · Export
              
      
              The header exports Markdown notes for All papers, Marked ✓ only, everything except ✗, or the
        visible list. with PDFs first downloads open-access PDFs; Math rewrites equations as LaTeX (slower).
        Missing PDFs lists papers still without one, with links to get them.
              
    
              
    
              
      
              Optional · AI screening
              
      
              Above a result list, AI screening lets a local model check each title and abstract against your inclusion and
        exclusion criteria. It only suggests; your marks decide. Skip it if you screen by hand.
              
    
              
    
              
      
              Evidence Lens · Ask and Matrix
              
      
              Ask answers from the selected project. Citation chips open quotes, paper details and the PDF at its page.
        Run audit checks citations; the Audit filter shows sentences that warrant attention.
        Needs review collects flagged sentences and cells for accept, reject or edit.
              
      
              Matrix compares papers using your columns. Extract, inspect cells, review values and export Markdown, CSV or JSON.
        Remote actions show their evidence count and cost before you confirm sending.
