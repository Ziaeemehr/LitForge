Quick guide

1 · Find papers

Click + New and choose how to start:

  • Similar: a DOI, arXiv id, PDF or abstract. LitForge follows its references, the papers citing it, and papers close in meaning.
  • Search: keyword queries, one per line.
  • Import list: a .csv, .bib or .ris export from Elicit, Zotero and similar tools.
  • My PDFs: PDFs you already have; each is matched to its paper and its text is extracted.

Each search belongs to a project (one literature review). Searches are listed under Recent.

2 · Read the scores

Relevance
How close the paper is to your topic: text meaning, shared references and citation links.
Importance
Field-normalized citations, centrality in this set, recent attention and venue.
Review priority
The two combined; the list is sorted by it by default.

Badges: new not in the project before · pdf PDF in the library · open free copy online · seed the paper you started from. Click a paper to see the breakdown, abstract and full text.

3 · Mark what you keep

✓ relevant, ✗ not relevant. Shift-click marks a range; the bar above the list marks many at once. Marks belong to the project and survive new searches. More like my ✓ papers searches again from everything you kept.

4 · Export

The header exports Markdown notes for All papers, Marked ✓ only, everything except ✗, or the visible list. with PDFs first downloads open-access PDFs; Math rewrites equations as LaTeX (slower). Missing PDFs lists papers still without one, with links to get them.

Optional · AI screening

Above a result list, AI screening lets a local model check each title and abstract against your inclusion and exclusion criteria. It only suggests; your marks decide. Skip it if you screen by hand.
